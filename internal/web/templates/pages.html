Daily reconciliation with the payment provider

Webhooks get lost. Once a day we download the provider's list of transactions and compare it with our payments, one payment at a time (by payment_ref). Comparing only totals is not enough: a lost $10 payment and an extra $10 payment give equal totals but are two bugs.

In production this is the Billing team's job. Credits cares about the result: a refund found here must take back the credits and licenses.

Break itWhat happensWhat reconciliation shows
Customer pays, provider has the money, the webhook never reaches us. missing_ours: paid but got nothing. Fix: Replay webhook → credits granted.
Our DB has a payment the provider never charged (bug or fake webhook). missing_provider: rights without money. Manual review.
Provider refunds the last payment and does not tell us. status_mismatch: money returned, rights kept. Fix: Replay webhook → rights revoked.
